# Problem Description

A leading retailer uses rugged Zebra devices in its warehouses. Warehouse floors are noisy, and network connectivity is spotty, making voice interactions difficult to use reliably.

The retailer tried ChatGPT's voice model without success, then tried Android's speech-to-text on top of its existing app. That approach has also not worked well enough. As a result, floor teams struggle to use the devices for everyday warehouse tasks.

The desired experience should let workers use natural language, primarily through audio, with text input available when needed. Common use cases include:

- Reporting damage.
- Checking inventory or stock availability.
- Requesting inventory or replenishment.
- Finding the location of specific stock.
- Reporting vague or incomplete issues that may require clarification.

The core problem is enabling reliable, useful interactions on rugged Zebra devices despite warehouse noise, intermittent connectivity, and requests that may be informal or incomplete.
